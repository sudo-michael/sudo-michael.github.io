About

I am a final year PhD candidate at Simon Fraser University under the supervision of Dr. Mo Chen and Dr. Sharan Vaswani.

I’m broadly interested in Safe Reinforcement Learning and Reinforcement Learning Theory. In particular, I’m interested in how Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability analysis can be combined with Deep Reinforcement Learning methods and the convergence rates of (stochastic) policy graident methods for (C)MDPs.
