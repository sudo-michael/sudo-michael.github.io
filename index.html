About

I am a final year PhD candidate at Simon Fraser University under the supervision of Dr. Mo Chen and Dr. Sharan Vaswani. I’m broadly interested in Safe Reinforcement Learning. In particular, I’m interested in developing principled algorithms for Constrained Markov decision processes with last-iterate guarantees. I'm also interested in exploring how these methods can then be applied to Reinforcement Learning with Preferences methods.

Prior to this, I completed a B.Sc. (Joint Honours) in Mathematics and Computing Science at Simon Fraser University. In my spare time, I enjoy cycling around Vancouver.
